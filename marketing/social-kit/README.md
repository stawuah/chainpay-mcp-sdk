## Voice

Modeled on how Phantom talks: short fragments, lots of "you", plain crypto words, no explaining.

- **Do:** "Your agent hit a paywall. It paid." · "Pause anytime. Seriously." · "Limits are limits."
- **Don't:** jargon (*mandate*, *PDA*, *policy*), exclamation marks, emoji, "seamless", "revolutionize".
- One idea per card. Numbers on cards (5 USDC, ends Friday) are illustrative, not a funded demo.

## Visual rules

- Palette: ChainPay blue `#0052FF`, ink `#14213D`, white, pale blue `#EAF1FF` / `#C9DBFF`.
- Top-left lockup on every image is composited from the real brand files (`frontend/public/brand/`) and Inter Medium, never AI-drawn.
- The mascot is a white soft-3D robot with a navy visor and two blue dot eyes. No arms, no mouth.
- Partner marks (x402, Crossmint, MCP, Solana, USDC, EURC, PYUSD, USDG) were rendered from official logo files used as references and checked by eye. They identify integrations and supported assets; they do not imply a partnership.

## Before posting

- ChainPay runs on **Solana devnet**. Don't caption anything as mainnet or live money.
- x402 support is ChainPay's custom receipt-proof flow, not standard x402 v2 sponsored settlement.
- USDT and Wise are not supported, so they are not in the kit.

## Regenerating

Images were generated on [KIE.ai](https://kie.ai) with `gpt-image-2-5-flare-image-to-image` at 2K, then cropped and stamped locally.

1. Put the scripts from `_source/` in a scratch folder with `npm i sharp`, ImageMagick, and Inter (`extras/ttf/Inter-Medium.ttf`).
2. `node rast.mjs` turns official logos in `logos/` into reference PNGs.
3. `KIE_API_KEY=… PROMPTS=./prompts2.mjs node gen.mjs [id …]` generates into `~/Desktop/ChainPay Social/raw/` (about 10 credits per image).
4. `PROMPTS=./prompts2.mjs node compose.mjs` crops to exact sizes and stamps the logo; `node kit.mjs <this folder>` exports JPGs here.

Never commit the API key. The scripts read it from the environment only.
